# Launch Experience Review — Founder's Notebook

> **A founder-owned experiential gate** (release package §7), the interface counterpart to child
> play-testing and educator review. It is **not** delegable to an AI: reading rendered markup is the
> architectural view again, not a first-time user's five minutes. This worksheet only *structures* the
> walk — the judgement is the founder's.
>
> **How to use.** Open each live app and use it as a **first-time parent or child** would. No code, no
> repository, no docs — just the product. Keep this beside you; every time you stop and think *"what does
> this do?" · "why is this here?" · "this is too dense" · "this image doesn't fit" · "I expected something
> else"* — write it down. Those moments are where trust is strengthened or weakened.
>
> **The one question every app must answer:** *Does this feel worthy of representing The People's Home?*

## After the walk — turning notes into work

Once this is filled, the friction moments sort into three piles (and only then does production re-engage):

| Pile | Owner | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective defects** | production (me) | broken elements · placeholder · dead links · dense-text fixes · brand drift |
| **Experiential judgement** | founder | tone, worthiness, what "The People's Home" should feel like |
| **Deeper gaps** | §7 human gates | needs a child, an educator, or a historian — not a fix |

*Nothing here is pre-filled. The value of this review is your unaided first impression; keep it that way.*
